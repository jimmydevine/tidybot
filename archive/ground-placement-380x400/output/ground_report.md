# Full ground assembly placement

Generated by `python3 design/ground/place_modules.py`. Placement only; not fabrication geometry.

Body: **380 × 400 × 230 mm**, including A1 height. Proposed side-brush sweep expands horizontal travel outline to **389 × 416 mm**; actual side brush is unmeasured.

Bottom/core mate z=110 mm; core/cap mate z=165 mm; cap top z=175 mm. Main robot stays outside the 50 mm furniture gap.

Proposed clear debris cavity: 140 × 110 × 65 mm = **1 L gross**. The 0.5–0.75 L usable target leaves nominal allowance for freeboard and collection details; it is not established by a bounding box.

## Geometry checks

- 990 independent reservation pairs checked in 3D; no positive-volume overlaps.
- Reference parts fit their parent reservations; sibling references do not intersect. Parent/child volumes intentionally nest.
- Battery, computer and bin translation sweeps clear modeled stationary reservations. Bin seal must retract first.
- Station bottom separation: 30 mm minus 13 mm mating projection = **17 mm clearance**.
- Cap opening passes the scanner's entire reference footprint; 70 mm lift leaves **5 mm** above scanner top.
- Four panel blanks with overlap allowance: **205 × 215 mm** nominal spans; joints and strength unmodeled.
- Head/duct/bin routing reservations meet at faces. This is not a flow, sealing or pressure-loss calculation.

AABB reservation checks only; touching routing faces and parent containment intentional. Unmodeled structure, cables, fasteners, tolerances, loads, scan optics, full station hardware and fluid performance are not validated.

- Only owned rollers/filter/wheel dimensions, identified manufacturer envelopes, and clearly marked listing references are represented as part dimensions.
- Reservations include their internal reference parts; do not sum their volumes or masses. Unallocated gaps are for segmented structure, seals and harness routing, not validated free space.
- Structural seats/posts and panels are not solid modeled here; latch coordinates establish joint zones, not rated locks, through-hole patterns or an approved shared interface.
- Floor body envelope excludes the side brush sweep. Its unknown real size must be resolved before printing adjacent parts.
- Three caster reservations require compliant mounting and drive-wheel load control; contact geometry alone does not establish stability or traction.
- No total mass, center of gravity, cleaning performance, runtime, autonomous reliability or lifting ability is established. All propulsion work remains deferred.

## Individual reservation notes

- **Dirty-air duct corridor:** Outside routing reservation, not a promised clear bore or finished duct; meets head and drawer at faces.
- **Rear-pull bin + filter drawer:** Includes filter holder on left and rear evacuation port; slide rear after retracting clean-air seal face.
- **CBM blower / mount bay:** Blower upright: intake toward +x/filter, tangential outlet toward +y/rear. Mount and adapters to detail.
- **Clean-air plenum + seal release:** Fixed plenum beside drawer; retract its sealing face 3 mm toward -x before drawer slides rear. Include station-evacuation isolation here.
- **Rear exhaust turn / diffuser:** Reserve rear/upward discharge above floor; diffuser area and pressure loss unverified.
- **Wheel H-bridge / terminal bay:** Above dirty duct with a sealed separating deck; Cytron PCB reference below, terminal/cooling space provisional.
