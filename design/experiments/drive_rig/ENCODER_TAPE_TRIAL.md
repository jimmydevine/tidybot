# Tape-wrap carrier: failed centering trial

2026-09-12. **The owner reports that the holder enters the bore but wobbles,
needs more shaft length, and is difficult to keep centered.** This trial did
not establish usable alignment or retention. Do not print a second one or
extend the tape-supported shaft as the next step.

The owner proposes a metal rod in a motor hole now reported as 3.4 mm. See
[the metal-rod review and saved questions](ENCODER_METAL_ROD.md). The earlier
free rotation with the holder removed remains confirmed. Added drag, exact
gap, insertion depth and tape layers for this trial were not reported.
