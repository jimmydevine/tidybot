# Full ground assembly placement

Generated by `python3 design/ground/place_modules.py`. Placement only; not fabrication geometry.

Body: **275 × 275 × 178 mm**, including A1 height. Proposed side-brush sweep expands horizontal travel outline to **305 × 275 mm**; actual side brush is unmeasured.

Bottom/core mate z=68 mm; core/cap mate z=113 mm; cap top z=123 mm. Main robot stays outside the 50 mm furniture gap.

Proposed clear debris cavity: 133 × 122 × 52 mm = **0.844 L gross**. The 0.5–0.6 L usable target allows for freeboard and collection details; it is not established by a bounding box.

## Geometry checks

- 903 independent reservation pairs checked in 3D; no positive-volume overlaps.
- Reference parts fit their parent reservations; sibling references do not intersect. Parent/child volumes intentionally nest.
- Battery, computer and bin translation sweeps clear modeled stationary reservations. Bin seal must retract first.
- Station bottom separation: 50 mm minus 37 mm mating projection = **13 mm clearance**.
- Cap opening passes the scanner's entire reference footprint; 70 mm lift leaves **5 mm** above scanner top.
- Whole main panels: **270 × 270 mm**; actual brim, toolpath, flatness and strength unverified.
- Rigid body and wheels fit275x275mm; every rigid part stays below180mm. Flexible bristles are explicitly exempt. Tool projections above the68mm mating plane fit reserved core openings.
- Head/duct/bin routing reservations meet at faces. This is not a flow, sealing or pressure-loss calculation.

AABB reservation checks only; touching routing faces and parent containment intentional. Unmodeled structure, cables, fasteners, tolerances, loads, scan optics, full station hardware and fluid performance are not validated.

## Individual reservation notes

- **Roller drive above cassette roof:** Continuation of the cassette side drive space; counted once as a drive assembly. Gives 62mm overall head-drive allowance, not a selected motor.
