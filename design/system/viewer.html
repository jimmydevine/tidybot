What remains uncertain

These are preliminary hardware allocations. No weight, traction, cleaning or flight result here is a physical test.

Custom locks, power protection, brush interfaces, telescoping seals and flight guards still need detailed qualification. A clean bounding-box check does not release a part for printing.

The owner considers the 644 × 1444 mm lift excessive; these layouts remain comparisons pending a compact redesign. Dusting now targets stopped ceiling fans about 7 ft high, 1 ft below the ceiling, with about 2 ft tool reach. This viewer retains the earlier wheeled duster. The revision D airborne study replaces it with a wheel-less bottom, raised lightweight boom and round-trip flight budget. The longer sofa telescope remains required.
